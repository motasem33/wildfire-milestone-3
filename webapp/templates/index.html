05 / METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Methodological decisions to keep in view

01

Label coverage

We excluded 378 observations from 2024 and the first 12 days of 2025 because all available labels were negative. This may indicate incomplete coverage; it does not prove that no fires occurred.

02

Temporal leakage control

Previous-weather summaries were rebuilt using shift(1). Preprocessing was fitted inside each training and validation pipeline.

03

Scope of use

The model uses same-day weather to classify a same-day label. It does not establish pre-event prediction or operational early-warning capability.

04

Temporal distribution shift

The positive-label rate rises from about 31% in training to 46% in the holdout, which limits direct generalization to future periods.
